# NovaRetail Internal IT Support Handbook

## VPN Access
Employees working outside the office must use the company VPN before accessing internal systems.  
- Install the **NovaSecure VPN client** from the internal Software Portal.  
- Sign in with your company email and password, complete MFA, and select the closest corporate gateway.  
- If the connection fails:
  - Confirm internet connectivity.
  - Restart the client.
  - Try another corporate gateway.  
- Do **not** disable endpoint security or firewall settings to make the VPN work.  
- If the problem remains, open an IT ticket with a screenshot of the error.

---

## Password Reset Policy
- Company passwords must be at least **14 characters**.  
- Use the **self-service identity portal** for normal password resets.  
- After **five failed login attempts**, the account may be temporarily locked.  
- If self-service recovery fails, contact the **IT Service Desk**.  
- Employees must **never share passwords or MFA codes** with support personnel.

---

## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- MFA is mandatory for **email, VPN, cloud administration, and other protected systems**.  
- Approved methods:
  - Company authenticator app  
  - Registered hardware security keys  
- SMS should only be used when explicitly enabled as a recovery method.

---

## Software Installation
- Install business software only from the **company Software Portal**.  
- Requests for software not listed require **manager approval** and **IT security review**.  
- Administrator access is **not granted** simply to install unapproved applications.

---

## Lost or Stolen Laptop
- Immediately report a lost or stolen laptop to the **IT Service Desk** and your manager.  
- Provide:
  - Device asset tag (if available)  
  - Last known location  
- IT will initiate **device lock/wipe procedures** and coordinate with Security.  
- Do **not** attempt to remotely recover the device yourself.

---

## Email Usage Policy
- Use company email only for **business purposes**.  
- Do not forward company emails to personal accounts.  
- Report suspicious emails to **IT Security** using the “Report Phishing” option.  
- Attachments from unknown senders must not be opened.

---

## Data Handling & Storage
- Store sensitive files only on **approved cloud storage** or company servers.  
- Do not use personal USB drives for company data.  
- Encrypt confidential documents before sharing externally.  
- Follow data retention policies — delete files only after approval.

---

## Remote Work Security
- Always connect through the **VPN** when working remotely.  
- Lock your device when unattended.  
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi without a secure hotspot or VPN.  
- Keep operating system and security patches up to date.

---

## Incident Reporting
- Report all IT incidents (security alerts, suspicious activity, hardware failure) immediately.  
- Use the **IT Service Desk portal** or call the hotline.  
- Provide screenshots, error logs, or detailed descriptions to speed resolution.

---

## IT Equipment Policy
- Company-issued equipment must be used responsibly.  
- Do not install personal software or games.  
- Hardware upgrades or repairs must be performed by IT only.  
- Return all equipment when leaving the company.
