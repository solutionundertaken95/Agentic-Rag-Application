# HR — Employee Benefits & Leave Policy (CONFIDENTIAL — HR Department)

**Document ID:** HR-BEN-2024-01 | **Department:** HR | **Classification:** Confidential

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

- Full-time employees accrue 24 PTO days per year, accrued monthly at 2 days/month.
- PTO must be requested via the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance.
- Unused PTO up to 8 days carries over to Q1 of the next year; the rest is forfeited.

## Sick Leave

- 12 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required for absences of 3+ consecutive days.
- Sick leave does not carry over.

## Parental Leave

- Primary caregiver: 18 weeks fully paid. Secondary caregiver: 4 weeks fully paid.
- Parental leave must be coordinated with HR at least 8 weeks before the expected date.

## Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

- Free, confidential counselling: 6 sessions per issue per year, via the external EAP provider.
- Contact hr-support@company.com for enrollment. Managers are not informed of EAP usage.

## Salary & Compensation Confidentiality

- Compensation details are strictly confidential. Sharing salary information outside HR-approved
  channels is a policy violation handled by HR and People Operations.
