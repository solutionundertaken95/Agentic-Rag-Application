# Finance — Payroll & Salary Bands (CONFIDENTIAL — Finance/HR Leadership Only)

**Document ID:** FIN-PAY-2024-04 | **Department:** FINANCE | **Classification:** Confidential

## Salary Bands (Annual, USD)

- Engineer I: 65,000–85,000 | Engineer II: 85,000–110,000 | Senior Engineer: 110,000–140,000
- Manager: 120,000–150,000 | Director: 160,000–210,000
- Bands are reviewed each October by Finance and HR leadership.

## Payroll Calendar

- Payroll runs on the 25th of each month (or the previous business day).
- Payroll queries: payroll@company.com. Pay-slips are available in the HR portal on pay day.
- Timesheet corrections must be submitted before the 20th; late entries roll to next month.

## Bonus & Variable Pay

- Annual performance bonus target: 10% of base for individual contributors, 15% for managers.
- Bonus payout is in March, pro-rated for mid-year joiners.
- Bonus details are individual and confidential; managers may not disclose another employee's bonus.
