# Finance — Expense Reimbursement & Procurement Policy (CONFIDENTIAL — Finance Department)

**Document ID:** FIN-EXP-2024-02 | **Department:** Finance | **Classification:** Confidential

## Expense Reimbursement

- Monthly expense claims are submitted via the finance portal by the 5th of the following month.
- Receipts are mandatory for any expense above $25.
- Meal allowance while travelling: $60/day domestic, $85/day international.
- Reimbursements are processed in the next payroll cycle after Finance approval.

## Certification & Training Reimbursement

- Job-related certifications are reimbursed up to $2,500 per employee per year.
- Approval chain: line manager → department head → Finance.
- The certification must be completed with a passing score; failed exam fees are not reimbursed.

## Purchase Orders & Procurement

- Purchases above $1,000 require a purchase order raised in the ERP before ordering.
- Purchases above $10,000 require dual approval: Finance Controller + CFO.
- Preferred-vendor list is maintained by Procurement; off-list purchases need a waiver.

## Budget Access

- Department budget actuals are restricted. Department heads may request reports from
  finance-reporting@company.com; individual employee compensation data is never shared.
